   - **Comprehensive focus styles:**

     * Buttons: 2px solid outline with ring offset

     * Custom components: Checkboxes, radio buttons, dropdowns, tabs

   - **High contrast mode:** Enhanced focus indicators (3px instead of 2px)
   - **Reduced motion:** Respects user's motion preferences
